# Ad to Pro · Scriptation

Five ranked ideas for Scriptation's onboarding and paywall, plus two versions of the flow side by side: one for people who searched "Actors, get off book faster!" on the App Store, one for "New draft? No problem.". Prepared by Adapty.

**→ https://eddieatadapty.github.io/scriptation-flow-rebuild/**

Opens on the Overview. `?open=proto` lands on the prototype; `&step=<screen>` opens both phones on one screen.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Let them try what the ad showed, on your sample script, before the plans | CR +10–20% to purchase |
| 02 | Ask their role once, then lead the plans with it | CR +8–20% to purchase |
| 03 | Put the period and the renewal next to every price | CR +3–8% to purchase |
| 04 | Catch the close with your lowest Industry Pro price | CR +3–8% to purchase |
| 05 | Offer a reminder before it renews | CR +3–8% to purchase |

## What differs by keyword

| Screen | "Actors, get off book faster!" | "New draft? No problem." |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| The ad | The actor video: “actors do not do this”, then Playback. Install now | The crew video: paper revisions on set, then Transfer Annotations. See details |
| App Store | Screenshots lead with “Run lines and self-tape” and “Listen to scripts with lifelike voices” (hypothesis) | Screenshots lead with “Transfer notes and save hours of prep” and “Compare drafts” (hypothesis) |
| Welcome | “Actors, get off book faster.” over your Run lines screenshot (hypothesis) | “New draft? No problem.” over your marked-up Draft 1 (hypothesis) |
| Your role | Actors already picked, from the ad | Nothing picked: the crew roles lead the list |
| Try it | Rehearse as Valerie on Draft 1: her lines highlighted, the other parts read aloud | Transfer Annotations from Draft 1 to Draft 2, then the notes on the new pages |
| Plans | “Get off book faster.” Actor tools first, plus Scriptation AI for the AI voices | “Every note, in every new draft.” Advanced Note Transfer first, then the tools of the role they pick |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Scriptation's App Store listing, website or in-app screens; Apple Ads figures from Adapty's research. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for Scriptation.
