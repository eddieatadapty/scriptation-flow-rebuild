INT. SOUNDSTAGE - SPACESHIP SET - MORNING

Harried and under-slept CREW bustle about a standing set. ROB, a production assistant, distributes sides to CAST. VALERIE, in a captain’s uniform, highlights her lines.

The soundstage door swings open, revealing 1st AD, DANIELLA, on the brink of losing it. Clipboard in hand, she addresses the crew:

DANIELLA

I just got word that another script revision is on the way.

ROB

You’re kidding.

DANIELLA

I wish I was.

The script supervisor, TIM, pokes his head out from under a mountain of multi-colored pages.

TIM

Are you sure?

DANIELLA

Quite sure, I’m afraid.

VALERIE

What did you hear? Lots of changes?

DANIELLA

Well, I don’t want to alarm anyone, but...

Daniella takes a deep breath.

DANIELLA (CONT’D)

It’s 36 pages.

Rob tosses his stack of sides into a nearby recycling bin and kicks it, injuring his foot.

Tim’s eyes well up with tears.

TIM

The script itself is only 30 pages!

VALERIE

Including title pages!

Rob limps to the Production Office to print new scripts.
